ScannerAz privacy policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how DRECOM LLC handles information when a seller uses ScannerAz. It applies to the ScannerAz mobile application, its authorized backend services, and this website.

Information ScannerAz processes

  • ScannerAz account and support details that a user provides.
  • Amazon selling partner authorization data, including a seller identifier, marketplace identifier, and OAuth refresh token when a seller chooses to connect Amazon.
  • Product search inputs and seller-facing results needed to provide product eligibility, catalog, offer, and price-review features.
  • Operational metadata such as request identifiers, timestamps, and security events needed to operate and protect the service.

How ScannerAz uses information

ScannerAz uses this information only to provide the features a seller requests, secure the service, investigate operational issues, and meet legal or contractual obligations. ScannerAz does not sell Amazon Information or use it for advertising.

Storage and protection

Production Amazon refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and are stored only in the server-side connection store. The mobile application does not receive Amazon client secrets or another seller's refresh token. Data in transit is protected by HTTPS.

Sharing

ScannerAz shares information only with infrastructure providers needed to operate the service and with Amazon when required to complete the authorized SP-API workflow. ScannerAz does not share one selling partner's data with another selling partner.

Retention and deletion

ScannerAz retains information only while needed for the connected feature, security, support, or legal obligations. A seller can request that ScannerAz disconnect Amazon and delete associated authorization data. Confirmed deletion requests are processed within 30 days unless a longer period is required by law.

Do not submit Amazon credentials, refresh tokens, card data, or buyer information through public support channels.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Contact Support.ScannerAz@drecom.dev or see the support page for data-deletion instructions.
