Account and data deletion

Last updated: September 25, 2026

A seller can request removal of their ScannerAz connection and associated Amazon authorization data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the ScannerAz support channel.
  2. Use the subject: ScannerAz data deletion request.
  3. Provide the ScannerAz account email or seller identifier needed to locate the connection. Do not send passwords, refresh tokens, API keys, or buyer information.
  4. ScannerAz will verify the requester before deleting data to prevent unauthorized removal.

What is deleted

After a validated request, ScannerAz revokes or disconnects the applicable Amazon connection and removes its stored encrypted refresh token and associated ScannerAz account data, unless retention is required by law or necessary for security incident records.

Timing

Confirmed deletion requests are processed within 30 days. If a request cannot be completed in that period, DRECOM LLC will provide a status explanation through the support channel.

Disconnecting an Amazon authorization may also be available directly in Amazon Seller Central. Use both the ScannerAz request and Amazon's controls when you want to immediately revoke app access.
